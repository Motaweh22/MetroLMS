## 1. Stage 0 — Preparation, Inventory & Governance Rules

### 1.1 Field Approval Governance Legend
- **`OWNER-APPROVED`**: Business requirement/attribute explicitly established by `D-OWNER-REV-01`–`33` or `CP-009` Erratum *(exact physical column name/SQL type remains reviewable at Checkpoint 1)*.
- **`PROPOSED`**: Newly drafted field or representation awaiting explicit Owner review.
- **`TECHNICAL-RECOMMENDATION`**: Engineering recommendation (SQL Server type, length, normalization, or surrogate key) with justification; never silently promoted to `OWNER-APPROVED`.
- **`UNRESOLVED-BUSINESS-RULE`**: Blocked on Owner business clarification (none open in Cluster 1).
- **`DEFERRED`**: Outside `WP-5.5` core scope (`D-OWNER-REV-33` / `API_ONLY`).

### 4.2 `INV-R4` Owner Clarification & Consolidation into `INV-R1`–`INV-R3` (`OWNER-APPROVED`)
- **Authoritative Source Comparison:**
  - `INV-R1` (`security-and-xss-baseline.md` §6, L98): `Developer` accounts cannot be created via any UI or public API endpoint (CLI/management-command bootstrap only).
  - `INV-R2` (`security-and-xss-baseline.md` §6, L99): No user can assign, promote, or demote any account to or from the `Developer` role via UI or API.
  - `INV-R3` (`security-and-xss-baseline.md` §6, L100): `Developer` accounts cannot be deleted, suspended, or deactivated via UI or API.
  - `INV-R4` (`security-and-xss-baseline.md` §6, L101 & `domain-business-rules.md` §1, L23–24): Stated that `Protected Admin` (`isProtectedAdmin: true`) cannot be deleted, demoted, or deactivated by `Developer` or peer `Admin` accounts.
  - `INV-R5` & `INV-R8` (`security-and-xss-baseline.md` §6, L102, L105): `Admin` is blocked from mutating `Developer`, and `Developer` identity is invisible to all non-Developer roles across all 11 confidentiality surfaces.
- **Owner Clarification & Consolidation Decision:**
  - The Owner confirmed that the intended protected account is the existing **`Developer` account** (`role = 'developer'`), with no separate Protected Admin account, role, or identity.
  - Because `INV-R2` and `INV-R3` already prohibit **any** actor (including `Developer` itself) from deleting, suspending, deactivating, or demoting a `Developer` account via UI or API, `INV-R4` introduces **no distinct protection behavior** beyond `INV-R1`–`INV-R3` (and `INV-R5`/`INV-R8`).
  - **Result:** `INV-R4` is **consolidated into / redundant with** the existing `Developer` protection rules (`INV-R1`–`INV-R3`, `INV-R5`, `INV-R8`) with full traceability preserved. The proposed `User.is_protected_admin` field, singleton filtered unique index, and extra role `CHECK` constraint are **rejected / removed** from the `User` mapping (`§2.1`), avoiding duplicate authorization logic.

Delete `UserPermissionOverride`.
6. Explicitly delete `ServerSession`, `UserJobTitle`, and `UserOrganizationalDepartment` rows (plus Cluster 3 user-owned operational rows when mapped).
7. Delete the `User` row.
8. Verify post-delete invariants and commit; any failure rolls back the entire transaction atomically.

### 8.7 Revised `D-01` Governance Interpretation — Role-Aware & Record-Aware Policy (`OWNER-APPROVED`)
*(Replaces the prior blanket Cluster 3 "delete everything" assumption. Never apply a universal deletion rule solely because an entity contains a `User` foreign key. Approved by Owner at WP-5.5 Stage 3 / Cluster 3 review.)*

1. **Core Governance Principles:**
   - **Personal & unnecessary user-associated data** is eligible for deletion.
   - **Organizational, operational, historical, and security records** may require preservation, anonymization, reassignment, or `DeletedUserTombstone` attribution.
   - **Both the user's role and the record's business purpose/dependencies** determine the outcome. Multi-user or shared records must never be deleted in a way that damages other users' records or organizational operations.
2. **Three Distinct `D-01` Operations (Never Interchangeable):**
   - **Operation A — Account Deletion:** Permanently removes/disables the `User` authentication record (`User` + `ServerSession` + `UserPermissionOverride`) and prevents future access (`DeletedUserTombstone` + `DeletedUserAlias` anti-resurrection), strictly respecting protected-account rules (`INV-R1`–`INV-R8`, `D-03`, `D-11`).
   - **Operation B — Personal Data Deletion:** Removes personal, mutable, or user-private operational records that no longer need retention (e.g., `UserOrganizationalDepartment`, `UserJobTitle`, `GroupMembership`, `GroupJoinRequest`, `NotificationItem`, `UserWhatsAppVisibility`, and eligible learner progress/enrollment rows).
   - **Operation C — Historical & Organizational Record Preservation:** Preserves records required for institutional continuity, security, governance, compliance, and auditing—either unchanged (system/shared records), **reassigned** (`Course.primary_lecturer_id` per `D-03`/`D-11`), **anonymized** (`CourseComment` per `D-02`/`CP-011`), or **transferred to `DeletedUserTombstone`** (`HistoricalServiceTrainingRecord`, `DelegatedAuthority`, `CourseReviewSubmission` per `CP-010`/`CP-011`).

### 8.10 Shared & Multi-User Record Handling & Controlled Reassignment (`PROPOSED`)
1. **Courses Created by Lecturers & Used by Trainees (`D-03`, `D-11`):** Deleting a `lecturer` **never** cascades to `Course`, `CourseDraft`, `PublishedCourseVersion`, `Module`, `LessonItem`, `Enrollment`, or trainee `CourseAttempt`s. `D-01` is **blocked (`409`)** until an authorized administrator (`admin`, `super_admin`, or `developer`—never `assistant_admin` alone) executes a controlled ownership reassignment of `Course.primary_lecturer_id` to an explicitly chosen active `lecturer` or `admin` (no fabricated default replacement owner; `originalAuthorNameSnapshot` preserved per `D-11`).
2. **`CourseAssistantLecturer` & `GroupAssistantAssignment`:** Removing one assigned assistant deletes only that user's assignment row; the `Course`, `TrainingGroup`, primary owner, and all other assistants remain intact.
3. **Training Groups & Group Resources (`TrainingGroup`, `GroupRenewalCycle`, `GroupCourseAssignment`, `GroupQrCode`, `GroupResource`):** Removing one `GroupMembership` deletes only that trainee's membership; all other members, cycles, QR codes, and meeting links remain unaffected.
4. **Enrollments & Multi-User Training Records:** Deleting one trainee's `Enrollment` / `EnrollmentSourceGrant` / `CourseAttempt` / `LessonProgress` affects only that trainee; `Course`, `PublishedCourseVersion`, `TrainingGroup`, and peer trainees' records are isolated.
5. **Course Reviews & Administrative Approvals (`CourseReviewSubmission`, `PendingApprovalRequest`):** Two-party/multi-party governance records are **never** deleted when one party is deleted (`CourseReviewSubmission` transfers to `DeletedUserTombstone` per `CP-011`; `PendingApprovalRequest` preserves snapshots and auto-cancels only `Pending` requests from a deleted requester).
6. **`DelegatedAuthority` (Grantor / Grantee):** Deactivated (`is_active = 0`) and Tombstone-attributed for the deleted party while preserving the other party's identity (`CP-010`).
7. **Scheduled Jobs (`ScheduledJob`, `ScheduledJobRun`):** System-owned operational records; completely insulated from any `User` deletion.

### 8.12 Proposed `D-01` Atomic Execution & Technical Integrity Workflow (`PROPOSED`)
*(Preserves SQL Server + Django `ON DELETE NO ACTION`, zero DB cascades, and single-transaction atomicity via `django.db.transaction.atomic()`. Verified against all `User.id` FKs across Clusters 1–3.)*

1. **Pre-Flight Validation Gate (Fail-Closed Before Any Mutation):**
   - Reject (`403`) if target `User.role == 'developer'` (`INV-R1`–`R3`) or actor lacks role authority (`INV-R2`, `INV-R3`, `INV-R5`, `INV-R6`, or `INV-R9` approval required for `assistant_admin`).
   - Reject (`409`) if target `User` is `Course.primary_lecturer_id` on any `Course` (`D-03`, `D-11` — require explicit admin reassignment first) OR if `WhatsAppContact.phone_e164` holds the deleted staff member's personal number requiring `admin`/`super_admin`/`developer` replacement/removal first.
   - Verify `PendingApprovalRequest` (`INV-R10`) execution-time re-validation if triggered via approval workflow.
2. **Atomic Transaction (`transaction.atomic()` on SQL Server):**
   - **Step 1 — Access Cutoff:** Delete `ServerSession` rows for `user_id`; delete `UserPermissionOverride`.
   - **Step 2 — Tombstone Creation:** Insert `DeletedUserTombstone` (`canonical_user_id`, `deleted_at`, `deleted_by_actor_id`, `reason`) and `DeletedUserAlias` rows.
   - **Step 3 — Tombstone Transfers & Deactivations (`CP-010` / `CP-011` / Cluster 2 Attribution):** Update `HistoricalServiceTrainingRecord` (`user_id = NULL, tombstone_id = T`, if retained per `CP-010`), `DelegatedAuthority` (grantor/grantee → Tombstone, `is_active = 0`), `CourseReviewSubmission` (submitter/reviewer → Tombstone), and the 4 `OWNER-APPROVED` authoring/publishing/uploader XOR attribution pairs (`Course.created_by_user_id` → `created_by_tombstone_id`, `CourseDraft.updated_by_user_id` → `updated_by_tombstone_id`, `PublishedCourseVersion.published_by_user_id` → `published_by_tombstone_id`, `MediaAsset.uploaded_by_user_id` → `uploaded_by_tombstone_id`).
   - **Step 4 — Anonymizations, Snapshot Updates & Shared-Asset Decoupling:**
     - Anonymize `CourseComment` (`author_user_id = NULL, is_author_deleted = 1`) and `CourseCommentReport` (`reporter_user_id = NULL`, report preserved).
     - Auto-cancel open `Pending` `PendingApprovalRequest` rows requested by `user_id` (`requested_by_id` non-FK snapshot preserved); decided/reviewer/executor non-FK snapshots remain untouched.
     - Verify `Course.primary_lecturer_id` reassignment was already completed in pre-flight (never left pointing to deleted `User`) and `MediaAsset` binaries/metadata remain untouched (`No Automatic Purge`).
   - **Step 5 — Leaf-to-Parent Personal Data Cleanup (`ON DELETE NO ACTION` Safe Order):**
     1. `UserWhatsAppVisibility`, `NotificationItem`, `GroupJoinRequest`, `GroupMembership`, `GroupAssistantAssignment`, `CourseAssistantLecturer`
     2. `UserJobTitle` → `UserOrganizationalDepartment` *(composite FK order)*
     3. Delete unissued/incomplete `CertificateRecord` rows; **decouple/nullify `course_attempt_id` on issued `CertificateRecord` rows** (`OWNER-APPROVED` preservation without auto-revocation) → delete `LessonProgress` → `CourseAttempt` → `EnrollmentSourceGrant` → `Enrollment`
   - **Step 6 — Delete `User` Row & Record Immutable `AuditLogEntry`:**
     - Delete `User` row (succeeds cleanly once all `ON DELETE NO ACTION` FKs are cleared/deleted).
     - Insert `AuditLogEntry` (`D-01`) inside the **same** `transaction.atomic()` block. **`ON DELETE NO ACTION` Compatibility:** `AuditLogEntry` (§2.9, §3) has **no FK** to `User.id` (`actor_user_id` and `target_entity_id` are `NVARCHAR(64)` non-FK snapshots + `details_snapshot_json` + `actor_role_snapshot` + `is_developer_masked`), so existing `AuditLogEntry` rows referencing the deleted user remain valid and appending the `D-01` audit row after `DELETE FROM User` is valid, attributing the deleted account via `target_entity_id` + Tombstone reference and the deleting actor via `actor_user_id`.
   - **Failure / Partial Execution Prevention:** Any `IntegrityError`, lock timeout, or validation failure rolls back the entire SQL Server transaction atomically—leaving zero partial deletions, orphaned sessions, or unlogged tombstones.

### 9.1 Detailed Approved Designs (`TR-C3-02` through `TR-C3-05`)
- **`TR-C3-02` (`GroupRenewalCycle.cycle_status` — `OWNER-APPROVED`):**
  - *Five Canonical States:* `Scheduled` (created, `start_datetime > now`), `Active` (started, in progress), `Completed` (finished successfully), `Expired` (deadline passed without completion), `Cancelled` (explicit cancellation of a future cycle before start).
  - *Transitions:* `Scheduled → Active | Cancelled | Expired` *(system overdue recovery)*; `Active → Completed | Expired`. Terminal states (`Completed`, `Expired`, `Cancelled`) allow no arbitrary transitions. Disabling `auto_renewal_enabled` prevents subsequent cycle creation without auto-cancelling an already-created future cycle; `Expired` remains strictly distinct from `Cancelled`.
  - *Overdue `Scheduled → Expired` Recovery (`OWNER-APPROVED`):* If a `Scheduled` cycle's `start_datetime` and `deadline_datetime` have both passed before the renewal job runs (e.g., due to scheduler outage, per `INV-DOM20-06`), execute **direct `Scheduled → Expired`** (rather than synthetic `Scheduled → Active → Expired`). Transition must be idempotent, preserve terminal immutability, and log the missed-window overdue recovery in `ScheduledJobRun` and `AuditLogEntry` without fabricating an `Active` period that never occurred.
