## 1. Domain Overview & Mission

- **Domain Mission:** Define the hierarchical structure of MetroLMS course content (Chapters/Modules and Lesson Items), lesson item types, decoupled media reference contracts (`D-MEDIA-01`), XSS-safe rich text rules (`D-SEC-01`), and the distinction between substantive content fields and metadata-only fields.
- **Domain Boundaries:**
  - *In Scope:* `Chapter` and `ChapterItem` hierarchy, supported item types (`video`, `pdf`, `quiz`, `text`, `assignment`), `MediaAssetReference` consumption (`D-MEDIA-01`), substantive vs. metadata field classification (`Rule B`), and DOMPurify sanitization boundaries (`D-SEC-01`).
  - *Out of Scope:* Video player progress tracking, sequential lesson locking, and quiz attempt grading (Phase 3 Domains 12–14), physical media storage/streaming server selection (`D-MEDIA-01` deferred to Phase 4/7/12), and CDN/DRM (none planned per `CL-02`/`CL-03`).
- **Actors & Responsibilities:**
  - *Course Author (`lecturer` / `admin`):* Organizes chapters, orders lesson items, uploads media via decoupled `MediaAssetReference`, and authors text/quiz items.
  - *Trainee (`user`):* Consumes published lesson items according to enrollment and sequential gates (Phase 3).
- **Core Entities:** `Chapter` (`Module`), `ChapterItem` (`LessonItem`), `MediaAssetReference`, `QuizDefinition`.

## 2. Formal Invariants & Business Constraints

| Invariant ID | Rule Title | Formal Logic Expression | Enforcement Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`INV-DOM06-01`** | **Decoupled Media Descriptor (`D-MEDIA-01`)** | Lesson media references must use `MediaAssetReference` (`assetId`, `url`, `mimeType`, `fileName`, `sizeBytes?`, `disposition?`). Components never hardcode storage paths, UNC shares, or vendor SDKs. | Media Service & `ChapterItem` |
| **`INV-DOM06-02`** | **Substantive vs. Metadata Field Split (`Rule B`)** | Substantive fields are strictly `{ type, videoUrl, pdfUrl, mediaId, content, durationSeconds }`. Metadata-only fields are `{ title, description, order, duration }`. | `computeItemSubstantiveFingerprint` |
| **`INV-DOM06-03`** | **Rich-Text XSS Sanitization (`D-SEC-01`)** | All rich HTML lesson notes must pass through the centralized DOMPurify allowlist (`sanitizeHtml.ts`); `<script>`, `<iframe>`, `on*` handlers, and `javascript:` URIs are stripped. | Frontend Sanitizer & Django Backend |
| **`INV-DOM06-04`** | **Active Attempt Deletion Guard (`REQ-CRS-04`)** | Published lessons associated with active trainee attempts cannot be silently deleted in-place without going through `Rule B` draft/review versioning. | Course Service |

## 3. State Transition Rules

- **Lesson Item Lifecycle:**
  - Authored/reordered in `Draft` $\rightarrow$ Snapshotted with `contentVersion` and `contentFingerprint` upon approved publication $\rightarrow$ Immutable inside historical `PublishedCourseVersion` snapshots.
- **Forbidden Transitions:**
  - Mutating `contentVersion` or `contentFingerprint` of a published item directly from client draft payloads.

  - *Domain 05:* Manages draft editing of chapters and items.

- **Confirmed Decisions vs. Unresolved Owner Questions:**
  - *Confirmed:* `D-MEDIA-01` (decoupled contract), `D-SEC-01` (DOMPurify allowlist), `Rule B` field split, **`INV-DOM21-07` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — media assets associated with a `PublishedCourseVersion` are binary- and identity-immutable; server-side enforcement is mandatory across all applicable upload and reference-update operations)**, **`INV-DOM21-12` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — media replacement uses the Two-Step Upload & Reference Repoint workflow: Step 1 uploads via Django `multipart/form-data` and creates a new asset with a new `assetId` without modifying any draft/content reference; Step 2 separately repoints only the target reference after independently revalidating target authorization, lifecycle state, replacement eligibility, and `assetId` validity; replacement completes only after Step 2 succeeds; not one atomic transaction—if Step 1 succeeds and Step 2 fails, the old reference remains unchanged and the new asset is not auto-deleted)**, **`INV-DOM21-13` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — display properties `title` and `isVisible` belong to the specific content/material usage reference rather than the underlying Media Asset; does not extend to Asset-level technical metadata `fileName`/`mimeType`/`sizeBytes` or prevent new Draft Course Versions / future cycles from having independent metadata)**, **`INV-DOM21-15` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — immediate `isVisible: true -> false` enforcement applies specifically to Active / Unsealed `GroupRenewalCycle` references and never hides or alters course/lesson references sharing the same Media Asset, nor extends to `PublishedCourseVersion`s)**, **`INV-DOM21-16` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — Asset-level technical metadata `fileName`, `mimeType`, and `sizeBytes` become immutable immediately after asset creation across all lifecycle states and reference counts, established from server-validated uploaded binary properties; binary replacement creates a new Media Asset with a new `assetId` and its own technical metadata per `INV-DOM21-09` and `INV-DOM21-12`; narrowly supersedes `API-REC-07` only regarding post-creation mutation of `fileName`, `mimeType`, and `sizeBytes`, with exact revised `PUT` semantics unresolved)**, **`CL-02` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — both Cairo Metro internal network and external internet/home/mobile access supported securely over HTTPS with Django session/authorization; never exposing SMB/Windows shares or physical paths; no CDN/public binary URLs approved)**, **Phase 1 Media Delivery (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — initial production uses Django-mediated, session-authenticated streaming via the Storage Abstraction with `Range`/`206`/`416` requirements, no separate Media Delivery Service in Phase 1, and known concurrent-playback worker/bandwidth risk requiring workload validation)**, **`CL-03` (`OWNER APPROVED`, `2026-11-13` — clarified as Future Media Authorization Direction; This applies **only** to Google Drive-hosted resources (never to MetroLMS-owned storage) and does not require Google Drive OAuth, temporary/signed URLs, or server-side video proxying.
