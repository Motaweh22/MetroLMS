## 6. Summary of What Was Completed

1. **Multi-Source Enrollment Provenance (`T-2.1.1`, `D-06`, `MFR-41`):**
   - Refactored `EnrollmentRecord` in `frontend/src/core/courses/types.ts` and `inMemoryStore.ts` to maintain `sources: EnrollmentSourceGrant[]` with unique deterministic `sourceId` and `attemptId` values.
   - Standardized grant formats: standalone catalog self-enrollment (`self:catalog:${attemptId}`), group-cycle assignment (`group:${groupId}:${cycleId}`), and continuation grants.
   - Enforced deterministic attempt ID generation (`ATT-${canonicalUserId}-${courseId}-${cycleId || 'standalone'}`) preventing collisions between distinct logical attempts.
   - Resolved prefix-overlap collisions in `resolveGroupAttemptId()` and `isCycleOwnedByGroup()` through structural delimiter checking and group-cycle ledger matching.
   - Implemented idempotent grant upserts in `addOrUpdateEnrollmentGrant()` preserving immutable attempt associations upon retries.
   - Authored Suites 1–5 (19 test specifications) in `frontend/src/test/enrollment-provenance.test.ts`.

2. **Per-Attempt Progress Isolation (`T-2.1.2`, `D-06`, `MFR-02`, `MFR-41`):**
   - Refactored authoritative progress storage from shared `userProgress[userId][courseId]` to isolated `attemptProgress[attemptId]`.
   - Introduced `AttemptProgress` domain entity and migrated `CourseAttempt` to synchronize directly with `attemptProgress` on every activity and completion mutation, eliminating dual-store divergence.
   - Implemented `resolveActiveAttemptForLearning()` with prioritized resolution (explicit `attemptId` $\rightarrow$ explicit `groupId` $\rightarrow$ single active grant $\rightarrow$ standalone fallback).
   - Ensured group renewal cycles provision fresh `CourseAttempt` instances at 0% while preserving historical attempts and completed lesson records.
   - Maintained lecturer and administrator preview mode isolation, guaranteeing preview completions never mutate trainee records.
   - Authored Suite 6 (12 test specifications) in `frontend/src/test/enrollment-provenance.test.ts`.

3. **Unambiguous Attempt Resolution & Context Selector Modal (`T-2.1.3`, `MFR-41`, §9.1):**
   - Eliminated the silent in-progress/latest-grant heuristic from authoritative attempt resolution.
   - Implemented structured ambiguity contract: when multiple eligible attempts exist without explicit context, `getCourseProgress` returns `{ requiresAttemptSelection: true, eligibleAttempts: EligibleAttemptOption[] }`, and unselected mutations throw `AttemptAmbiguityError` (HTTP 409, `AMBIGUOUS_ATTEMPT_CONTEXT`).
   - Created accessible, Arabic-first `AttemptContextSelectorModal` component in `LearningPlayerPage.tsx` displaying human-readable labels (`تسجيل ذاتي مستقل`, `مجموعة: ...`), cycle titles, progress bars, and last activity timestamps without raw technical IDs.
   - Propagated selected `attemptId` end-to-end across URL search parameters (`?attemptId=<id>`), player hooks, API services, navigation transitions, and MSW handlers.
   - Enforced `CourseAccessMode` (`full` vs `review_only`): attempts post-deadline under `keep_no_cert` or archived cycles grant `review_only` access; mutations fail closed with HTTP 403 `COURSE_ACCESS_REVIEW_ONLY`.
   - Authored Suite 7 (16 test specifications) in `frontend/src/test/enrollment-provenance.test.ts`.

4. **Standalone Attempt Provision & Progress Reader Unification (`T-2.1.4`, `MFR-32`, `MFR-33`):**
   - Enforced automatic standalone `CourseAttempt` and `attemptProgress` provisioning upon self-enrollment (`enrollUser`) and grant updates (`addOrUpdateEnrollmentGrant`) (`MFR-32`).
   - Updated `getCoursePerformanceReport` to evaluate both group and standalone attempts for all enrolled trainees using `calculateTraineeProgress`, eliminating 0% reporting for self-enrolled trainees (`MFR-32`).
   - Updated `getUserTranscript` to remove the sticky `isCompleted = true` latch and `Math.max` guard across attempts, authoritatively computing current attempt progress via `calculateTraineeProgress` intersected with published `flatItems` (`MFR-33`).
   - Unified all progress readers (`getCourseProgress`, `getGroupMetrics`, `getCoursePerformanceReport`, `getUserTranscript`, `getMyEnrolledCourses`) onto authoritative `calculateTraineeProgress`.
   - Restricted legacy `userProgress` import in `getCourseProgress` to single-eligible contexts, preventing ambiguous progress attribution.
   - Authored Suite 8 (16 test specifications) in `frontend/src/test/enrollment-provenance.test.ts`.

## 9. Architecture / Contract Changes

- Established `EnrollmentRecord.sources: EnrollmentSourceGrant[]` as the canonical enrollment provenance model.
- Standardized `attemptProgress[attemptId]` as the sole authoritative mutable progress store.
- Replaced silent in-progress/latest heuristics with typed structured ambiguity responses.
- Bound all Learning Player operations, telemetry, and completion mutations to explicit `attemptId`.
- Unified `getCourseProgress`, `getGroupMetrics`, `getCoursePerformanceReport`, and `getUserTranscript` onto `calculateTraineeProgress`.

---

## 10. Requirements or Business Rules Clarified

- Standalone attempts possess `groupId: null, cycleId: null` and are isolated from group cycles.
- Idempotent grant retries preserve bound `attemptId` without re-provisioning or overwriting attempts.
- Enrolled trainees without an existing attempt record automatically resolve or provision a standalone attempt upon report generation or progress querying.
- `getUserTranscript` strictly evaluates the trainee's active attempt and dynamically recalculates progress when curriculum expands.
